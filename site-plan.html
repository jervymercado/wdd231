Site Plan: Metro Manila Ice Skating Community Hub

Site Name

Metro Manila Ice Skating Community Hub — the name was chosen because it clearly tells visitors what the site is about and who it's for.

Site Purpose

The site brings together rink schedules, rates, and lesson/event information for ice skaters in Metro Manila, all in one place.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find a rink near me and today's schedule?
  • How do I sign up for beginner lessons or events?

Color Scheme

Ice Blue (#1B98E0) — headings. Deep Navy (#0B2545) — body text. White (#FFFFFF) — background. This document uses these colors.

Typography

Poppins — used for headings. Roboto — used for body text (as shown on this page).
